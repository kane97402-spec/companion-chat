Local privacy

Chats, study sets, and preferences are saved in this browser's local storage. They are not synced to an account or other devices. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to see them.

Character portraits are loaded from public anime databases when available. Voice input and read-aloud depend on browser support. Video lessons use AI-generated scripts with browser-based animation and optional speech. They are not cinematic footage or MP4 exports.
