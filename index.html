What works in this version and where your data lives.

No AI is connected

Chat replies are clearly marked demo responses. There is no API key, AI provider, backend, or paid AI usage in this version.

Local privacy

Chats, study sets, and preferences are saved in this browser's local storage. They are not synced to an account or other devices. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to see them.

Character portraits are loaded from public anime databases when available. Voice input and read-aloud depend on browser support. Video generation and live AI voice chat are not included.
